First release of ZPay, an offline Android app for de-escalating street solicitation or extortion. It looks like a UPI payment app, cannot move money, and every payment ends in a banking failure.

## What's included

- **Payment flow:** GPay-style home screen, live UPI QR scanner, payment page and UPI PIN page.
- **Four failure timings per account:** Before PIN, After PIN, Long Delay and Indefinite.
- **Six failure types:** server unavailable (U16), insufficient funds (Z9), recipient's bank down (U28), daily limit reached (U69), account frozen (XK) and payment not allowed (U30).
- **Hidden configuration:** bank profiles for 34 Indian banks plus custom ones, launch route, theme, connection delay and Hidden Pay Button Zones.
- **Disguise:** the launcher name can show as ZPay, QuickScan QR or Scanner Utility.

## Safety and privacy

- No success state exists anywhere in the app.
- Fully offline, with the internet permission removed.
- Nothing about a payment attempt is saved. The session clears when you leave the app.
- Screenshots, screen recording and the Recents preview are blocked.
- Settings are encrypted, Android app backup is off, and Emergency Wipe erases everything.
- Biometric Unlock is on by default.
